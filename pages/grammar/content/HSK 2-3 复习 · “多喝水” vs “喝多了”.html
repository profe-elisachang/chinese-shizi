多喝水 vs 喝多了
Two small words, opposite timing — a HSK 2–3 grammar comparison
HSK 2–3 · Grammar Review
多喝水
Advice
Cup still filling — hasn't happened yet
→ 前 = 建议
before verb = advice
喝多了
Result
Cup overflowed — already too much
Not done yet · a plan
多 + Verb
Means "do more of it" — it's advice or a suggestion about the future. Nothing has happened yet.
多喝水  ·  多运动  ·  多吃水果
Already happened · too much
Verb + 多了
Means "did too much of it" — it's a finished action with a result, usually not a good one.
喝多了  ·  吃多了  ·  跑多了
多 before the verb = advice for later  ·  多 after the verb (as 多了) = it already happened, and it was too much.
Advice
多喝水
"Drink more water" — good for your health, do this from now on
Result
水喝多了
"(I) drank too much water" — already done, stomach feels full
Advice
少抽烟
"Smoke less" — 少 (less) works the same way as 多, placed before the verb
Result
烟抽多了
"(He) smoked too much" — already happened, now he's coughing

📖 Quick rule (English)
🔹 多 + V = “do more (advice)”   (future / suggestion)
🔹 V + 多了 = “did too much (result)”   (already happened / overdone)
💡 对……不好 = “bad for …” (review)
