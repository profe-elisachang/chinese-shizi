课文 我想去北京（一）
1 / 4
课文正文
我是在 1997 年喜欢上汉语、开始学习汉语的。那年，我刚考上大学dà xué，学校里新开了中文系xì，每个学生都可以学习汉语。虽然我知道汉字对我们外国人来说很难nán，而且写汉字就像画画huà huà一样，但我觉得非常有意思yì si，决定jué dìng先学几个月试一试shì yi shì。
Vocabulary
画画 huà huà v.
To draw; to paint
Wǒ xǐhuān zài xīngqītiān huà huà.
I like to paint on Sundays.
决定 jué dìng v.
To decide
Wǒ juédìng xīngqīyī qù Běijīng.
I decided to go to Beijing on Monday.
试一试 shì yi shì v.
To give it a try; to test
Zhè kǒu hěn hǎochī, nǐ shì yi shì.
This tastes great, give it a try!
Translation I became interested in Chinese and began studying it in 1997. That year, I had just been admitted to university, and a Chinese department had just opened at my school. Every student could study Chinese. Although I knew that Chinese characters would be very difficult for us foreigners, and that writing them was like drawing pictures, I found them very interesting, so I decided to study Chinese for a few months and give it a try.
课文 我想去北京（二）

课文正文
中文系有从中国来的老师，也有我们巴基斯坦bā jī sī tǎn的老师，中国老师大多来自北京，而ér我们国家的老师呢，也都是从北京的大学毕业的。不管bù guǎn是在课上，还是hái shi课下，他们经常谈到tán dào北京。
Vocabulary
巴基斯坦 bā jī sī tǎn n.
Pakistan
而 ér conj.
Whereas; on the other hand
Tā xǐhuān chá, ér wǒ xǐhuān kāfēi.
He likes tea, whereas I prefer coffee.
不管...还是... bù guǎn... hái shi... conj.
No matter whether... or...
谈到 tán dào v.
To talk about; to mention
Translation The Chinese department had teachers from China as well as teachers from Pakistan. Most of the Chinese teachers came from Beijing, while our teachers from Pakistan had all graduated from universities in Beijing. Whether in class or outside of class, they often talked about Beijing.

课文正文
从他们的谈话tán huà中，我渐渐jiàn jiàn了解liǎo jiě了北京，知道北京很漂亮，文化很丰富fēng fù，有很多名胜古迹míng shèng gǔ jì，等等。
Vocabulary
谈话 tán huà n.
Conversation; talk
渐渐 jiàn jiàn adv.
Gradually; step by step
Tiānqì jiànjiàn biàn lěng le.
The weather is gradually turning cold.
了解 liǎo jiě v.
To understand; to learn about
丰富 fēng fù adj.
Rich; abundant
名胜古迹 míng shèng gǔ jì n.
Places of historic interest and scenic beauty
Translation From their conversations, I gradually learned about Beijing. I found out that it is very beautiful, has a rich culture, and is home to many famous scenic and historic sites, among other things.
课文 我想去北京（四）

课文正文
我们的课本上也有一些谈到北京的课文，我对那些名胜古迹míng shèng gǔ jì的照片特别感兴趣gǎn xìng qù。就这样，我喜欢上了北京，爱上了北京。我很希望能到北京去，去看看故宫gù gōng、长城cháng chéng、颐和园yí hé yuán。
Vocabulary
名胜古迹 míng shèng gǔ jì n.
Places of historic interest and scenic beauty
感兴趣 gǎn xìng qù v.
To be interested in
Wǒ duì Zhōngguó wénhuà hěn gǎn xìngqù.
I am very interested in Chinese culture.
故宫 / 长城 / 颐和园 n.
The Forbidden City / The Great Wall / The Summer Palace
Translation Our textbooks also contained some passages about Beijing, and I was especially interested in the photos of its famous scenic and historic sites. That was how I came to love Beijing. I really hoped to visit and see the Forbidden City, the Great Wall, and the Summer Palace.
