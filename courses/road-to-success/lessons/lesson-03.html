给父母的一封信

你现在还写信吗？你用汉语写过信吗？ 语法与课文
亲爱的爸爸、妈妈：
Dear Dad and Mom:
Queridos papá y mamá:
你们好！
How are you?
¡Hola! ¿Cómo están?
我来北京学习汉语快半年了，这是我第一次用汉语写信。看到我的这封中文信，你们一定很高兴吧？
I have been in Beijing studying Chinese for almost half a year now, and this is the first time I am writing a letter in Chinese. You must be very happy to see this letter in Chinese from me, right?
Llevo casi medio año en Beijing estudiando chino, y esta es la primera vez que escribo una carta en chino. Deben de estar muy contentos al ver esta carta en chino mía, ¿verdad?
我刚从中国的南方旅行回来。这次我去了很多地方，有上海、苏州、杭州，还有桂林和阳朔。以前我在汉语课本里学过“上有天堂，下有苏杭”这句话，现在我才真正理解了它的意思。桂林和阳朔的风景也美极了，人们都说“桂林山水甲天下”。
I have just come back from a trip to southern China. This time I went to many places: Shanghai, Suzhou, Hangzhou, and also Guilin and Yangshuo. I had learned the saying “There is paradise above, and Suzhou and Hangzhou below” in my Chinese textbook before, but only now do I truly understand what it means. The scenery in Guilin and Yangshuo is extremely beautiful, too. People all say, “Guilin’s mountains and waters are the finest under heaven.”
Acabo de volver de un viaje por el sur de China. Esta vez fui a muchos lugares: Shanghái, Suzhou, Hangzhou, y también Guilin y Yangshuo. Antes había aprendido en el libro de texto el dicho «Arriba está el paraíso y abajo, Suzhou y Hangzhou», pero recién ahora entiendo de verdad lo que significa. El paisaje de Guilin y Yangshuo también es hermosísimo. Todo el mundo dice: «Las montañas y las aguas de Guilin son las mejores bajo el cielo».
这次去旅行，我的收获很多。不仅欣赏了美丽的自然风光，而且还了解了一些当地的风俗习惯。另外，还吃到了很多好吃的东西，特别是各地风味不同的小吃。虽然都是南方人，苏杭一带的人口味比较清淡，但是桂林人比较喜欢吃辣的，口味要重一些。
I gained a lot from this trip. Not only did I enjoy the beautiful natural scenery, I also learned about some local customs. In addition, I ate a lot of delicious food, especially snacks with different local flavors. Although they are all southerners, people in the Suzhou–Hangzhou area prefer lighter tastes, while people in Guilin like spicy food and stronger flavors.
Gané mucho con este viaje. No solo disfruté del hermoso paisaje natural, sino que también conocí algunas costumbres locales. Además, comí muchas cosas ricas, sobre todo aperitivos de distintos sabores locales. Aunque todos son del sur, a la gente de la zona de Suzhou y Hangzhou le gustan los sabores más suaves, mientras que a la gente de Guilin le gusta más lo picante y los sabores más intensos.
这次去旅行，我的另外一个收获就是被邀请去中国人家里过年，感受到了中国人过年时的热闹、欢乐的气氛。那是我旅行到桂林的时候，正赶上中国最大的节日——春节。过春节的时候，中国人一定要全家人聚在一起吃团圆饭。看到我独自一人，导游就热情地邀请我去他家。因为他的爸爸是北方人，妈妈是南方人，所以我们又包饺子，又吃年糕。他还带我一起去给他的亲戚、朋友拜年。这让我特别感动。
Another thing I gained from this trip was being invited to spend the New Year in a Chinese family, and I felt the lively, joyful atmosphere of Chinese New Year. That was when I was traveling in Guilin, and I happened to be there during China’s biggest festival—the Spring Festival. During Spring Festival, Chinese people always get the whole family together for a reunion dinner. Seeing that I was alone, the tour guide warmly invited me to his home. Because his father is from the north and his mother is from the south, we both wrapped dumplings and ate New Year cake. He also took me along to pay New Year visits to his relatives and friends. This moved me especially.
Otro fruto de este viaje fue que me invitaron a pasar el Año Nuevo en casa de una familia china, y sentí el ambiente animado y alegre de esa fiesta. Fue cuando viajaba por Guilin y justo coincidí con la fiesta más importante de China: el Festival de Primavera. En esa fecha, las familias chinas se reúnen para la cena de reunión familiar. Al verme sola, el guía me invitó con entusiasmo a su casa. Como su padre es del norte y su mother del sur, hicimos tanto jiaozi como niangao. También me llevó a felicitar el Año Nuevo a sus parientes y amigos. Esto me emocionó de verdad.
好了，先写到这儿吧。爸爸，您觉得我现在的汉语怎么样了？这次旅行让我更想学好汉语，想更多地了解中国。以后我也要跟您一样，当一名汉语老师。
All right, I’ll stop here for now. Dad, how do you think my Chinese is now? This trip has made me want even more to learn Chinese well and to know China better. In the future I also want to be a Chinese teacher, just like you.
Bueno, por ahora lo dejo aquí. Papá, ¿qué te parece mi chino ahora? Este viaje me ha hecho querer aún más aprender bien el chino y conocer mejor China. En el futuro también quiero ser profesora de chino, igual que tú.
祝爸爸、妈妈身体健康！
爱你们的女儿　瑞贝卡
2月6日
Wishing Dad and Mom good health!
Your daughter who loves you, Rebecca
February 6
¡Les deseo a papá y a mamá buena salud!
Su hija que los quiere, Rebeca
6 de febrero

1. 理解 (lǐjiě) — understand / comprehend / comprender
📘 Structure: 理解 + 意思 / 问题 / 人 (understand a meaning, a problem, or a person)
“理解” 表示了解事情的意义或懂得了某人的想法，包含深层的体会与包容。 "理解" means grasping the deeper meaning of something or understanding someone's thoughts. "理解" significa comprender el sentido profundo de algo o entender los pensamientos de alguien.
① 我非常理解你的心情。
I understand your feelings very well.
Entiendo muy bien cómo te sientes.
② 读了这封信，我才真正理解了“上有天堂，下有苏杭”的意思。
After reading this letter, I truly understood the saying "There is paradise above, and Suzhou and Hangzhou below."
Tras leer esta carta, entendí verdaderamente el dicho «Arriba el paraíso, abajo Suzhou y Hangzhou».
辨析 Comparison: “理解” vs “明白/懂得”
“明白”侧重于清楚事实；“理解”侧重于深刻懂得原因、道理或情感。
2. 感受 (gǎnshòu) — feel / experience / sentir
📘 Structure: 感受 + 气氛 / 热情 / 快乐 (feel/experience atmosphere, warmth, or joy)
动词或名词。指接触客观事物后产生的体会或心理反应。 Verb or noun. To feel or experience emotionally/sensorially; or an impression/feeling. Verbo o sustantivo. Sentir, experimentar o la impresión/sensación experimentada.
① 在桂林过年，我感受到了中国人过年时的热闹气氛。
Spending New Year in Guilin, I felt the lively atmosphere of the Chinese New Year.
Pasando el Año Nuevo en Guilin, sentí el ambiente animado del Año Nuevo Chino.
② 请你谈一谈在中国生活的感受。(名词 Noun)
Please talk about your feelings/impressions of living in China.
Por favor, habla de tus impresiones de vivir en China.
3. 收获 (shōuhuò) — harvest / gains / logros; frutos
📘 Structure: 有很多收获 / 收获很大 / 收获知识与友情
原指农作物成熟后收割，引申为取得的成果或收益。 Originally refers to agricultural harvest; extended to mean knowledge, experience, or fruits of effort gained. Originalmente la cosecha agrícola; por extensión, los conocimientos o ganancias fruto del esfuerzo.
① 这次旅行我的收获很大，不仅学会了做饺子，还交到了新朋友。
I gained a lot from this trip: not only did I learn to make dumplings, but I also made new friends.
Gané mucho con este viaje: no solo aprendí a hacer jiaozi, sino que también hice nuevos amigos.
4. 词根对比：风光 / 风俗 / 风味
自然风光 (fēngguāng) Scenery / Landscape / Paisaje natural

桂林的自然风光非常独特。

风俗习惯 (fēngsú) Customs / Costumbres

各地有不同的过年风俗。

地方风味 (fēngwèi) Local flavor / Sabor local

我非常喜欢吃桂林风味的小吃。

5. 独自 (dúlì / dúzì) — alone / by oneself / en solo
📘 Structure: 独自 + 一人 / 旅行 / 生活 / 完成
① 看到我独自一人，导游热情地邀请我去他家过年。
Seeing that I was alone, the tour guide warmly invited me to his house for New Year.
Al verme sola, el guía me invitó con entusiasmo a su casa para Año Nuevo.
1. 快……了 (kuài... le) — Almost / Soon
表示动作或状态很快就会发生，或者时间即将达到某一数量。
Indicates that an event is about to happen or a time period is almost reached.
① 我来北京学习汉语快半年了。(时间即将满半年)
I've been in Beijing studying Chinese for almost half a year.
② 火车快开走了，我们快点儿走吧！
The train is about to leave, let's hurry up!
2. 不仅……而且…… (bùjǐn... érqiě...) — Not only... but also...
递进复句关联词。主语相同时，“不仅”放在主语后；主语不同时，“不仅”放在主语前。
Used to express progressive relationship. "Not only... but also..."When the subjects are the same, "not only" is placed after the subject; when the subjects are different, "不仅" is placed before the subject..
① 我不仅欣赏了美丽的自然风光，而且还了解了当地的风俗习惯。
I not only enjoyed the beautiful natural scenery, but also learned about local customs.
② 这家饭馆的小吃不仅便宜，而且非常好吃。
The snacks at this restaurant are not only cheap, but also delicious.
3. 另外 (lìngwài) — In addition / Besides / Another
① 连词 Conjunction：表示在前面说的之外补充说明 (In addition / Besides)。
Indicates additional information beyond what was mentioned above.
① 欣赏了风光，另外，还吃到了很多小吃。
(Conjunction) Enjoyed the scenery; in addition, ate many snacks.
这个周末我想去看个朋友，另外，还想跟马丁去看场电影。
This weekend I want to visit a friend. In addition, I also want to go see a movie with Martin.
② 代词/形容词 Pronoun/Adj：表示范围之外的 indicates something additional or outside the group (another / other).
② 这次去旅行，我的另外一个收获就是去中国人家里过年。
(Pronoun) Another harvest of my trip was celebrating New Year at a Chinese home.
③ 他只告诉了我这些，另外的事情他没说。
He only told me these things; he didn't mention anything else.
④ 就剩这一张报纸了，另外的都被他们拿走了。
Only this newspaper is left; they took all the others.
⑤ 老师说，明天上课时再给我们讲另外一个问题。
The teacher said they would explain another question to us in class tomorrow.
⑥ 你们几个先坐火车走，另外的人明天坐飞机去。
A few of you go by train first; the others will fly tomorrow.
③ 副词 Adverb：表示超出上文所说的范围，常和“还”“再”“又”等副词连用。
Means "in addition to what has been mentioned" and often appears with adverbs such as 还, 再, and 又.
① 这本《汉英词典》送给你吧，我另外还有一本。
You can have this Chinese-English dictionary; I have another one as well.
② 快上课了，这件事我们另外再找时间商量吧。
Class is about to start. Let's find another time to discuss this matter.
③ 他又另外找了几个人帮忙，才把这个大箱子搬走。
He found a few more people to help, and only then did they manage to move the big box.
4. 正赶上 (zhèng gǎnshàng) — Happen to coincide with
恰好遇到某种机会、情况或节日。
To happen to encounter a specific event, opportunity, or festival.
① 我旅行到桂林的时候，正赶上中国最大的节日——春节。
When I traveled to Guilin, it happened to be during China's biggest festival—Spring Festival.
